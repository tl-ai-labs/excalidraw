# Security Review — pass1

Scope: the whole of `packages/element/src/textDiff.ts` and `packages/element/src/textRangeMerge.ts`, against NFR-2 and the merge's diff budget. Ranges, origin texts and element fields are treated as attacker-controlled. Run `20261102-103113-feature-new-text-range-styling`, read from the working tree at HEAD `d1c04fda`.

## Summary

The diff itself is sound. No constructed input hung it, and the meter bound held over 1.95M fuzz cases. The surrounding merge does not meet NFR-2. Three inputs stop a client: a 1.2 KB message makes `reconcileElements` throw, a roughly 700 KB message makes it throw another way (the plan's unaccepted open defect, still present), and two deliveries totalling 2.2 MB leave an element that `JSON.stringify` cannot serialise. The cause of the third is that the cap bounds records, not bytes, and 1,024 records can each hold a copy of the same text. The meter also does not bound merge time: mapping records through hunks is unmetered and takes up to 18 times the rest of the merge. Result: 3 High, 1 Medium, 1 Low. Sign-off should wait for the High fixes.

## Passing checks

- Meter bound holds: 1,952,124 fuzz cases in `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-macbook-Documents-GitHub-Google-Projects-excalidraw/01d1c9f3-e157-4714-a8e0-b66e8dff9b17/scratchpad/sec8/c.mjs` with random meters, including lone surrogates, gave 0 overruns past `max(2n+1, a+b-1)`, largest ratio 0.667, and no charge with the meter at or below zero. These texts were short; the plan's own runs cover large ones.
- Same fuzz: 0 throws, hunks always rebuild the new text and stay in order, no edge inside a surrogate pair, mapped spans stay in bounds, and results are repeatable.
- No hang found: the search terminates (stride at least 2, every gap at least 3 characters), and the slowest single reconcile measured was 375.3 ms. The one crafted binary-search input took 4.3 ms.
- Odd string and number input does not throw: `__proto__` and `constructor` ids, `z` of 1e308, offsets of ±1e308, and origins of lone surrogates (`/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-macbook-Documents-GitHub-Google-Projects-excalidraw/01d1c9f3-e157-4714-a8e0-b66e8dff9b17/scratchpad/sec8/d.sec.test.ts`).
- Array or length-object `originalText` does not throw: `hashString` throws inside restore, and the catch at `restore.ts:1007` drops that element.
- PII, authn and authz, audit log, surface and headers: n/a, not in the touched set.
- Secrets: pattern has no hit in the two files. Dependencies: unchanged, no manifest or lockfile among the 31 touched files, so `npm audit` was not run.

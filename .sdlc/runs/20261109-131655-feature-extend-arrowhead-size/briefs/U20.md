3. 3. getArrowheadSize, getArrowheadAngle, getArrowheadSizeBeyondLastSegment and getCardinalityMarkerScale keep their signatures and bodies.
4. 4. New private `extendBoundsByArrowheads(element: ExcalidrawLinearElement, curve: Drawable, bounds: Bounds, transformXY: (p: GlobalPoint) => GlobalPoint): Bounds`, placed right before getLinearElementRotatedBounds.
5. 5. It returns `bounds` itself (no copy, no allocation) unless isArrowElement(element) and getArrowheadSizeScale(element.arrowheadSize) > 1.
6. 6. 7. 7. Each part calls getArrowheadPoints(element, [curve], position, partArrowhead, offset) and skips a null result.
8. 8. A circle part's result is [x, y, diameter]: transform pointFrom<GlobalPoint>(x, y) with transformXY and extend min/max by diameter * diameterScale / 2 on every side.
9. 9. Any other part's result is a flat x, y list: transform each consecutive pair with transformXY and extend min/max; return [minX, minY, maxX, maxY].
10. 10. In getLinearElementRotatedBounds, `coords` starts from extendBoundsByArrowheads(element, shape, [res[0], res[1], res[2], res[3]], transformXY); the bound-text branch after it is unchanged.

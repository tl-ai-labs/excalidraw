5. 5. When !appState.viewModeEnabled, render a delete button (data-testid={`page-delete-${page.id}`}, aria-label={t("pages.deletePage")}, disabled={appState.pages.length <= 1}) calling app.deletePage(page.id), and at the end of the bar render an add-page button (<button type="button" className="excalidraw-page-bar__add-btn" data-testid="page-bar-add" aria-label={t("pages.addPage")} onClick={() => app.addPage()}>+</button>).
6. 6. In view mode (appState.viewModeEnabled === true), hide or omit the add, rename, move, and delete controls while keeping page tabs clickable for switching pages.
